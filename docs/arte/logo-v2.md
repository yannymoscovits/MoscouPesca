# Logo MoscouFishing v2

Gerado com a ferramenta integrada image_gen, usando a imagem enviada pelo usuário como referência de composição. PNG com transparência, integrado à tela inicial em `src/assets/ui/moscou-fishing-logo-v2.png`. Os botões continuam sendo controles HTML.

## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand. Create a production-ready transparent PNG logo asset for an existing Brazilian pixel-art fishing game, inspired closely by the upper logo in the user's reference image (reference only, not a whole screen). Isolate ONLY the logo crest: a cheerful stocky elderly fisherman Adilson with white beard, glasses, brown fishing hat, blue shirt and olive fishing vest, holding a curved fishing rod, with a jumping Brazilian freshwater fish to his right, turquoise splash and a cyan circular water halo behind him. Integrate the big, exceptionally legible exact wordmark 'MoscouFishing' across the bottom foreground of the character: Moscou in ivory, Fishing in golden yellow, thick deep navy pixelated outline and a narrow cyan rim, as in the reference. Cohesive polished hand-crafted pixel art with crisp pixel edges and rich restrained shading, handsome face and friendly smile, matching the reference character's identity. Horizontal compact crest composition approximately 3:2 width-to-height, tightly framed but generous transparent margin to avoid clipping rod or letters. Entire character upper body, rod tip, fish, splash and full wordmark must fit. Actual transparent background outside the crest, including corners. No full-screen background, no buttons, no ribbon, no dedication, no slogan, no extra text, no watermark. This will be placed above live HTML game controls, not used as a screenshot.
